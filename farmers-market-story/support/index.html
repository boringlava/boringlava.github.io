Farmers Market Story Support

Boring Lava LLC

Need help with Farmers Market Story? Email support@boringlava.com. The quickest way is Settings › Privacy & Support › Email support in the Game, which fills in your platform and app version for you.

What to include

  • Your platform (iPhone/iPad or Android) and the app version.
  • For a purchase problem: the order or transaction ID from your Apple or Google Play receipt.
  • A short description of what happened.

Never send passwords, payment card numbers, or full receipts or screenshots of your payment details. We will never ask for them.

Purchases and refunds

  • Missing item after buying: reopen the Game while online; purchases are delivered automatically. If it is still missing, email us with the order or transaction ID.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play through Google Play refund help. When a refund is granted, any unspent gems or undelivered items from that purchase are removed from the Game.
