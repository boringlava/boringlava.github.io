Farmers Market Story Privacy Policy

Boring Lava LLC · Effective September 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Farmers Market Story mobile game ("the Game") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Boring Lava LLC, the Game's developer. If you have questions, email support@boringlava.com.

Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device and, if you enable it, in your Apple or Google cloud-save service.
  • Our servers keep a random installation ID and records needed for daily rewards, events, purchases and ad rewards.
  • Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device information under Google's own policy.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Game data stored on your device

Your board, inventory, progress, settings and similar gameplay state are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you use cloud saves.

Cloud saves

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the Game can store your save in that platform's saved-games service. That data is held by Apple or Google under their privacy policies; Boring Lava LLC does not operate a separate save-storage account for you.

Installation and player identifiers

When the Game connects to our servers it creates a random installation ID and session credentials. If you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, we receive a verified, platform-issued player identifier so that rewards and purchases can be recovered if you reinstall on the same platform. We do not receive your Apple or Google account email or password.

Rewards, events and purchases

To deliver daily rewards, event rewards and purchased items exactly once, our servers record reward claims, event participation and progress reported by the Game, and purchase transactions. For purchases we receive transaction information from Apple or Google (such as product, transaction ID, date, and refund status). Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Rewarded advertising

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and ad-interaction data, and may use it to serve, measure and personalize ads, subject to your device settings and any consent you give. On iOS, the Game asks for permission before any tracking as required by Apple's App Tracking Transparency. We receive confirmation that an ad was completed so we can grant the reward. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Diagnostics and gameplay analytics

If enabled, the Game sends limited diagnostic and gameplay events to our own servers, such as errors, tutorial and level progress, economy events, screen views, event participation, and ad or purchase outcomes. We do not collect screen recordings, screenshots, your saves, free-form text, or credentials through analytics. You can turn analytics off in the Game's settings.

Server logs

Like most online services, our servers and hosting provider (Amazon Web Services) automatically process technical information such as IP address, request time and app version to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

  • To run the Game, sync rewards and events, and deliver and restore purchases.
  • To grant rewards for completed ads and enforce fair limits.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and keep our servers secure.
  • To fix bugs and improve game balance and features.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, except as performed by Google AdMob when you choose to watch ads as described above. We share information only with:

  • Apple and Google, for platform sign-in, cloud saves and purchases.
  • Google AdMob, for rewarded ads.
  • Amazon Web Services, which hosts our servers.
  • Authorities or others when required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public, and as part of a merger or acquisition subject to this policy.

Data retention

  • Player-associated analytics records: up to 90 days. Anonymous aggregated statistics: up to 13 months.
  • Reward, purchase and account-binding records: for as long as needed to provide the Game, prevent duplicate rewards, handle refunds and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Server backups: rotated within about 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Analytics: turn off in the Game's settings.
  • Ad tracking: manage it in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Local data: uninstalling the Game removes data stored on your device.
  • Access or deletion: email support@boringlava.com to request a copy or deletion of data associated with your installation or player ID. We may need information from the Game (such as the ID shown in settings) to find your records. Some transaction records may be kept where the law requires.

Depending on where you live (for example California or Canada), you may have additional rights to know, access, correct or delete your personal information and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children

The Game is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), hashed credentials, access controls and encrypted storage to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

International users

The Game is offered in the United States and Canada. Information is processed and stored in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and, for material changes, notify you in the Game where appropriate.
