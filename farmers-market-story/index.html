About the game

From roadside stand to the heart of Main Street.

Farmers Market Story is the first game from Boring Lava Games. You start with a humble produce stand and, merge by merge, grow it into a thriving farmers market that brings the whole town back to life.

  1. Merge

    Merge fresh goods

    Combine produce, baked goods, flowers, honey and more to create better items, then serve the customers waiting at your market.

  2. Upgrade

    Grow your stalls

    Upgrade your market stalls for more storage and handy new tools as your business grows.

  3. Build

    Revive Main Street

    Reopen the old storefronts and fill the street with new stalls and vendors, one step at a time.

  4. Deliver

    Fill local orders

    Ship crates of goods to Dottie's Diner and the other Main Street businesses to earn rewards.

  5. Story

    A story that grows

    Meet Dottie and the people of the town as you go from a humble roadside stand to the center of the community.
