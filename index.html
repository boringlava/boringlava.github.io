Our first game

  • In development
  • Planned for iOS & Android
  • Cozy merge game

Farmers Market Story is a cozy merge game about growing a humble roadside produce stand into the heart of a bustling Main Street farmers market.

  • Merge fresh goods. Combine produce, baked goods, flowers, honey and more into better items, then serve the customers waiting at your stall.
  • Grow your stalls. Upgrade market stalls for more storage and handy new tools as your business grows.
  • Revive Main Street. Reopen the old storefronts and fill the street with new vendors, one step at a time.
  • A story that grows. Meet Dottie and the people of the town on the way from roadside stand to the center of the community.

About the company

About Boring Lava LLC

Boring Lava LLC is an independent software and game development company based in Washington, United States. Through our studio brand, Boring Lava Games, we build approachable mobile games with satisfying progression, charming presentation, and thoughtful design.

We're a new company, and Farmers Market Story is our first game. We'd rather make a few things well than many things quickly, so each project gets our full attention, from the first prototype to player support.

  • Satisfying progressionSteady, visible growth that makes every session feel worthwhile.
  • Charming presentationWarm art, friendly characters and small details worth noticing.
  • Thoughtful designClear rules, fair systems, and games that are easy to put down and pick back up.
